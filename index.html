MindShield adds a brief moment of reflection when you ask AI to do something you could easily think through yourself. It helps you stay actively involved while still using AI when it genuinely helps.

AI makes it easy to stop thinking.

AI can answer questions, write explanations, solve problems, and make decisions almost instantly. That's useful — but it's also easy to start asking AI for things you could have figured out yourself.

MindShield is designed to add a small amount of friction before that becomes a habit.

How MindShield works

A small pause when you need it. No interruption when you don't.

1

Write your prompt

Use your AI assistant normally. MindShield works in the background while you type.

2

MindShield evaluates it

An on-device model checks whether your request appears to be a simple shortcut or a more substantial task.

3

Pause or continue

Simple shortcuts can trigger a brief reflection period. More complex tasks can pass through immediately.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about MindShield.

What is MindShield? ▸

MindShield is a privacy-first browser extension designed to help you avoid unconscious AI dependence. By introducing a gentle, brief pause before simple queries, it encourages you to think independently while keeping AI readily available for genuine, complex work.

Does MindShield send my prompts anywhere? ▸

No. Never. Your prompt text never leaves your browser. Classification runs 100% locally on your computer via an on-device machine-learning model compiled to WebAssembly. There are no analytics, no telemetry servers, and no cloud loggers.

Does MindShield block AI? ▸

No. MindShield is not an AI blocker or website restrictor. It acts as an intentional speed bump. When a query is flagged as a basic shortcut, it simply pauses submission for a short reflection period. Once that moment passes, or if your prompt is substantial, you can submit immediately.

Why does MindShield pause some prompts? ▸

Repeatedly using AI for minor tasks can make it easier to rely on AI automatically rather than attempting the task yourself. Pausing simple shortcuts introduces just enough friction to create a brief moment of awareness: "Do I actually need AI for this, or can I quickly think through it myself?"

What is cognitive offloading? ▸

Cognitive offloading is the practice of delegating mental tasks—such as remembering, calculating, reasoning, or analyzing—to external digital tools. While it can be useful and efficient, researchers have raised questions about how repeated reliance on external tools may influence cognitive engagement and independent problem-solving over time.

Which AI assistants does it support? ▸

MindShield currently supports ChatGPT, Claude, Google Gemini, and Grok web interfaces, with ongoing updates to support more platforms.

Is MindShield open source? ▸

Yes. MindShield is entirely open source. You can inspect the classification logic, review extension permissions, or contribute directly on GitHub.

How does the local AI model work? ▸

MindShield packages a compact, optimized classifier compiled to WebAssembly (Wasm). It assesses the complexity, intent, and depth of your query in milliseconds right inside your browser tab—achieving fast, privacy-preserving evaluation without requiring an external API key.
