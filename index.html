MindShield

Use AI without letting it do your thinking for you.

MindShield adds a brief moment of reflection when you ask AI to do something you could easily think through yourself. It helps you stay actively involved while still using AI when it genuinely helps.

🦊 Firefox 🌐 Chrome ⭐ GitHub

Free · Privacy-first · Local AI · Open source

AI makes it easy to stop thinking.

AI can answer questions, write explanations, solve problems, and make decisions almost instantly. That's useful — but it's also easy to start asking AI for things you could have figured out yourself.

MindShield is designed to add a small amount of friction before that becomes a habit.

How MindShield works

A small pause when you need it. No interruption when you don't.

1

Write your prompt

Use your AI assistant normally. MindShield works in the background while you type.

2

MindShield evaluates it

An on-device model checks whether your request appears to be a simple shortcut or a more substantial task.

3

Pause or continue

Simple shortcuts can trigger a brief reflection period. More complex tasks can pass through immediately.

Designed to keep you thinking

MindShield is designed to support AI use, not eliminate it.

🧠 A speed bump for unnecessary AI use

When a prompt looks like a simple shortcut, MindShield introduces a short pause that gives you a chance to think before relying on AI.

⚡ Don't interrupt productive work

Complex prompts, educational requests, debugging, research, and other substantial tasks can bypass the reflection delay.

🔒 Your prompts stay on your device

Prompt evaluation uses an on-device machine-learning model. Your prompts aren't sent to an external server for classification.

🌐 Works across popular AI assistants

MindShield supports ChatGPT, Claude, Google Gemini, and Grok.

ChatGPT Claude Gemini Grok

AI should be a partner, not a replacement for thinking.

MindShield is built around that idea.

You don't need to stop using AI. You just don't want to automatically outsource every small decision, calculation, explanation, or problem to it.

MindShield creates a small moment where you can decide: "Do I actually need AI for this?"

Your prompts stay private.

MindShield performs prompt classification locally in your browser using a machine-learning model compiled for WebAssembly.

Your prompts are not sent to an external classification server.

No tracking. No analytics. No cloud prompt analysis.

The project is open source, so you can inspect how it works yourself.
